Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

home-nest is a personal tool used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Nest devices.

  • Data accessed: device status and settings (temperature, humidity, thermostat mode and setpoints) from the owner's Nest account, via the Google Smart Device Management API.
  • Use: the data is used only to display device status and apply settings the owner requests.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is stored on any server.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
